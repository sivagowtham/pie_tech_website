Our institution

Building technical capability and confidence

Pollachi Institute of Engineering and Technology (PIE Tech) was established in 2012. The institution describes its mission as providing competent technical education and preparing graduates to contribute to society.

PIE Tech is managed by Hayagreeva Educational and Charitable Trust, which also manages Pollachi College of Arts and Science. This redesigned page presents institutional information in a clearer, student-friendly format.

Please refer to the official institution and regulatory disclosures for the latest approved details, affiliation status and programme intake.
◎

Vision

To produce technically sound, competent and value-based graduates who can face global challenges and contribute to society and national development.

✦

Mission

To impart value-based education in science and engineering through effective teaching and learning supported by appropriate facilities.

Our approach

Encourage practical learning, student participation, research exposure and preparation for professional pathways.

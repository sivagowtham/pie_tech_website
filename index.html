Welcome

Welcome To Hayagreeva Educational and Charitable Trust

Pollachi Institute of Engineering and Technology (PIE Tech) is a premier educational institution managed by Hayagreeva Educational and Charitable Trust. The Trust manages one more institution namely Pollachi College of Arts and Science(PCAS). Read more

Pollachi Institute of Engineering & Technology (PIE Tech) was established in 2012 with a mission of providing competent technical education and creating proficient engineers who will add newer dimensions to the technical arena and thereby serve the need of the Indian Society as a whole. Its commitment is best encapsulated in its vision to become a globally recognized institute of engineering. Read more

Vision

To produce technically sound, competent and value based graduates to face the challenges of the world and contribute towards the development of the society and growth of our Nation.

Mission

Faculty

At PIE Tech, we have Faculty members, who are good, inspired and motivated teachers, who understand their importance in shaping the future of students. They seek new innovative approaches of instruction and inculcate involvement, participation and team work among students to create an environment conducive for learning.

Scholarships

It is the culture of PIE Tech to award and encourage students with good academic records. PIE Tech provides unlimited scholarships for Diploma & +2 students based on their academic performance.

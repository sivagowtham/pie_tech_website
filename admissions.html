Admission guidance

Understand your application route

The official PIE Tech admissions page states that the institution is AICTE-approved and affiliated to Anna University. It lists TNEA counselling code 2354. Applicants should verify current approvals, deadlines and eligibility before applying.

2354Published TNEA counselling code
+2Typical entry route for undergraduate programmes; verify current rules
2 routesSingle-window counselling and management enquiry

Before you apply

  • Confirm the programme and current eligibility requirements.
  • Check the latest TNEA schedule and official counselling instructions.
  • Keep academic and identity documents ready as specified by the current admission authority.
  • Contact PIE Tech directly for management-quota enquiries and current seat availability.
Admission schedules, application procedures and fees can change each academic year. This page is a guide, not an application submission or an official admission offer.

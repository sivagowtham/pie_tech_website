Financial support

Ask about current scholarship opportunities

The official PIE Tech website states that scholarships are offered based on academic performance for eligible Diploma and +2 students. Availability, eligibility criteria, award amounts and application dates should be confirmed for the current academic year.

Questions to ask before applying

  • Which scholarships are open for the current admission year?
  • What marks, income or community documents are required?
  • What is the application deadline and submission method?
  • Can a scholarship be combined with other eligible support?
  • When and how will decisions be communicated?
Do not rely on old scholarship claims or assume a scholarship is guaranteed. Obtain current written terms from the college.
